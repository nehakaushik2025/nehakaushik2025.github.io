About Jivilytics

Mission Statement

Our mission is to make high-quality, evidence-based healthcare accessible to every person, regardless of economic circumstance.

We are committed to putting patients first and creating healthcare solutions grounded in evidence-based medicine, clinical expertise, and trusted science. We use technology, including AI, thoughtfully and responsibly to help improve access, support better decisions, and enhance the delivery of care.

Our goal is simple: better healthcare, built on evidence, accessible to everyone.

Jivilytics is proudly Australian-founded and based. Our team brings together engineers and medical specialists in Sydney, Australia, united by a shared commitment to improving healthcare.

Our Leadership Team

Shweta Kaushik

Chief Medical Officer and Founder
Dr Shweta Kaushik is a Specialist in Glaucoma, Cataract Surgery and General Ophthalmology and a Fellow of the Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Ophthalmologists. She has a strong research background with a PhD and many international publications in peer reviewed journals. Her research includes the use of deep-learning and AI technologies for retinal screening, with a focus on improving early detection, diagnosis and access to specialist eye care. Shweta is also a Clinical Associate Lecturer with the University of Sydney and is involved in teaching and selection of medical students.

Neeraj Chandan

Chief Technology Officer and Founder
Neeraj Chandan is an experienced technology professional specialising in enterprise architecture, systems integration, cloud technologies, and the design and delivery of large-scale technology solutions. He has expanded his expertise into AI through further study and professional certification, with a growing focus on applying AI to healthcare and developing practical, evidence-driven technology solutions. Neeraj also holds an honorary position with NSW Health and recently completed a project supporting its healthcare initiatives.
